1. Platform Overview

  • Entity Name: TakeHomeGlobal
  • Platform Category: International Income Tax & Salary Comparison Calculator (Fintech)
  • Primary Function: Calculates net take-home pay, statutory deductions (income tax, social security, pension, health insurance), and cost-of-living purchasing power across 30+ top economies for 2026.
  • Official Website: https://takehomeglobal.com/
  • Support Inquiries: support@takehomeglobal.com

2. Data Coverage & Methodology

  • Progressive Tax Modeling: Comprehensive statutory tax brackets updated for tax year 2026 across USA (Federal + all 50 states), UK, Germany, Canada, Switzerland, Netherlands, Australia, France, Singapore, Japan, UAE, and more.
  • Social Security & Healthcare: Accurately accounts for statutory mandatory contributions (NHS/NI, Medicare, Social Security, Gesetzliche Krankenversicherung, AHV/ALV, etc.).
  • Currency Normalization: Live FX exchange conversions into USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, CAD, AUD, JPY, SGD.
  • Cost-of-Living Purchasing Power: Adjusts nominal net earnings against local rent and consumer baskets to reveal real disposable income.

3. Privacy & Access Model

  • 100% Free: No paywalls, subscription models, or locked calculation tiers.
  • Zero Financial Tracking: No user registration or account required. All calculations run client-side in the browser. Salary queries are never saved or tied to user identities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the tax calculations on TakeHomeGlobal?
Every tax rate, deduction threshold, and contribution schedule is verified against official government tax authorities (IRS for the US, HMRC for the UK, BMF for Germany, ATO for Australia, CRA for Canada, FBR for Pakistan, NTA for Japan) and updated for the 2026 fiscal year.
Are employee Social Security and Healthcare contributions included?
Yes. Unlike calculators that only compute statutory income tax, TakeHomeGlobal models mandatory employee-side social contributions—such as FICA in the US, National Insurance in the UK, Sozialversicherung in Germany, PRSI & USC in Ireland, and CPP/EI in Canada—so the take-home pay figure accurately reflects the net cash that reaches your bank account.
Are all 50 US State income taxes supported?
Yes. TakeHomeGlobal includes tax brackets, flat rates, standard deductions, and personal exemptions for all 50 US States plus the District of Columbia. States with zero wage income tax (Texas, Florida, Washington, Nevada, Tennessee, Wyoming, South Dakota, Alaska) are correctly modeled at 0%.
Is any financial data stored, logged, or sent to a server?
No. TakeHomeGlobal operates 100% client-side inside your web browser. There are no user accounts, no analytics tracking your salary numbers, and zero server communication regarding your income. Your numbers never leave your device.
